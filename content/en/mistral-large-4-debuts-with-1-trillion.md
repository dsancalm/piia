Mistral Large 4 is a 1 trillion-parameter natively multimodal model with 49 billion active parameters. Mistral trained it from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own European datacenters. The public preview is available now through Mistral Studio. Weights are scheduled for release by the end of October 2026. The model supports self-deployment on private cloud or on-premise, enabling sovereign and auditable AI under European law.

The performance numbers target enterprise workloads directly. On a real vulnerability reproduction and patching test, ML4 scores 82%, the highest of any model evaluated. It solves 93% of Cybench challenges, covering 40 security competition exercises. On DeepSWE v1.1 it reaches 61.7%, on SWE-Atlas-QnA 59.4%, and on Terminal-Bench 4.0 28.3%. The combined Coding Agent Index score is 49.8%, ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 and Qwen3.8 Max.

Visual grounding surpasses frontier closed models. ML4 scores 42% on Dense 200 compared to 41% for GPT-6-Astra. On AutomationBench, which covers 657 business workflows, ML4 scores 59.9%, outperforming Kimi K3, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, and DeepSeek V4 Pro. On AA-Briefcase for long-horizon knowledge work, it reaches 1,393 Elo, ahead of DeepSeek V4 Pro. In blind human evaluation via Surge AI, ML4 Preview ranks second of five models with a 3.74 average on a 1, 5 scale.

The model handles over 160 languages, including all official EU languages, and can generate a full Hartree, Fock simulation in one shot. Training data composition and post-training methodology remain undisclosed. Mistral states ML4 will serve as the foundation for a new generation of specialized models, though timeline and scope are not specified. Red-teaming with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities is ongoing; results are not publicly reported. Commercial pricing, licensing terms, and non-preview availability have not been announced.
